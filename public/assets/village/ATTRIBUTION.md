Village texture assets
======================

Downloaded free PBR texture sets from ambientCG for the village scene.

- PavingStones006, 1K JPG: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=PavingStones006
- Plaster001, 1K JPG: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plaster001
- Wood066, 1K JPG: https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood066

ambientCG assets are provided under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal license.

Roof tiles are generated procedurally in `src/main.js` because the first requested
ambientCG roofing download URL was unavailable during implementation.
